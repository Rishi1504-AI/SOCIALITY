Commercial Terms, 50/50 Payment Protocol & Refund Policy

handshake 1. Acceptance of Terms & Statement of Work (SOW)

By engaging Sociality AI ("Company", "We", "Us", or "Our") for custom web development, AI agent implementation, digital design, or consulting services, the Client ("You", "Client", or "Customer") agrees to be bound by this Master Services Agreement.

All projects commence strictly upon written approval of a formal Proposal, Statement of Work (SOW), or digital contract outlining the agreed scope, milestones, timelines, and deliverable specifications.

credit_score 2. The 50/50 Milestone Payment Structure

To guarantee dedicated engineering resources, zero compromise on architecture quality, and mutual alignment, all project engagements adhere to the standard 50/50 Milestone Payment Protocol:

1 50% Upfront Retainer (Pre-Development)

Due prior to sprint initialization, UI/UX wireframing, custom codebase architecture, server provisioning, or developer allocation. Non-negotiable prerequisite to commence work.

2 50% Final Settlement (Upon Completion)

Due upon successful staging demonstration, User Acceptance Testing (UAT), and before final production deployment, live domain handover, or source code repository transfer.

Late Payments & Project Holds: Invoices are payable within seven (7) calendar days of issuance. In the event of an overdue balance exceeding ten (10) calendar days, Sociality AI reserves the right to suspend active development sprints, pause cloud AI agent endpoints, and withhold final code delivery until all outstanding dues are settled in full.

currency_exchange 3. Comprehensive Refund & Cancellation Policy

Sociality AI provides bespoke, high-touch engineering and consulting services. Because custom digital products consume non-recoverable engineering hours, server resources, and proprietary architecture, refunds are governed under the following strictly defined conditions:

  • Discovery Phase Cancellation (Within 48 Hours): If the Client requests cancellation in writing within 48 hours of onboarding, provided no custom UI designs, wireframes, or code architecture have been initialized, a full refund of the initial 50% deposit will be granted, less a nominal 10% administrative and onboarding processing fee.
  • Active Development Phase: If a cancellation is requested after wireframes, prototypes, custom frontend components, or AI workflows have been built, the initial 50% deposit is non-refundable and will be retained by Sociality AI as fair compensation for committed labor and resource allocation.
  • Non-Refundable Third-Party Disbursements: Any third-party costs directly disbursed on behalf of the client — including domain registrations, third-party server hosting fees (e.g., AWS, Supabase, Vercel), telephony charges (Twilio, Retell, Vapi), LLM token consumption credits (OpenAI, Anthropic), or licensed typography/assets — are 100% non-refundable under all circumstances.
  • Post-Delivery & Final Sign-Off: Once the project has passed final demonstration, the second 50% milestone is settled, and the source code or administrative credentials have been transferred, no refunds will be issued under any circumstances.

tune 4. Scope Revisions & Change Requests

Any feature, integration, or design modification requested by the Client that exceeds the agreed deliverables defined in the initial SOW shall constitute a "Change Request."

Change Requests will be evaluated, quoted as an additional milestone or at an hourly consulting rate, and scheduled into development only upon mutual written approval. Delivery timelines will be adjusted accordingly.
