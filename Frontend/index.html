What services does Sociality AI provide?

add

We specialize in blending human creativity with AI efficiency. Our core services include AI-assisted digital product design, bespoke web development, branding with a focus on tactile modern aesthetics, and workflow optimization consulting for creative teams.

How do you integrate AI into the design process?

add

We use AI as a collaborative tool, not a replacement for human intuition. Our process involves using AI for rapid prototyping, data-driven layout testing, and generating foundational code structures, which our expert designers and developers then refine, craft, and polish to ensure a bespoke, high-quality final product.

What is your pricing model?

add

Our pricing is project-based, tailored to the specific scope and complexity of your needs. We provide transparent, detailed proposals after an initial consultation to ensure alignment on deliverables and value. We also offer retainer options for ongoing design and development support.

How long does a typical project take?

add

Timelines vary based on scope. A foundational branding and landing page project typically takes 3-5 weeks, while comprehensive web applications or full platform redesigns may span 8-12 weeks. We establish clear milestones and deliverable dates during the kickoff phase.

Do you offer post-launch support?

add

Yes, absolutely. We believe digital products are living entities. We offer ongoing maintenance, optimization, and feature expansion packages to ensure your platform continues to perform and evolve alongside your business.
