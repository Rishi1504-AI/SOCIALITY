apartment 1. Introduction & Data Fiduciary Identity

Welcome to Sociality AI ("Company", "We", "Us", or "Our"). We are an enterprise web engineering and autonomous AI agency headquartered in Solapur, Maharashtra, India.

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, India) and global privacy frameworks (including the General Data Protection Regulation / GDPR), Sociality AI acts as the Data Fiduciary (or Data Controller) when you interact with our website (sociality.ai), schedule discovery calls, or engage our custom software and AI development services.

This Privacy Policy details the types of personal and operational data we collect, our technical security practices, and our strict commitment to safeguarding your proprietary information.

inventory_2 2. Categories of Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary information required to deliver high-performance digital products, deploy customized AI agents, and provide ongoing technical support:

  • Directly Provided Contact & Inquiry Data: Your name, official business email address, phone number (+91 or international), company name, project requirements, budget parameters, and scheduling notes submitted via our discovery forms or Calendly integrations.
  • Automated Technical & Telemetry Data: IP address, browser type and version, device identifiers, operating system, referring URL, time-zone settings, page load latencies, and anonymized Core Web Vitals telemetry.
  • AI Agent Interaction Data (When using deployed AI systems):
    • WhatsApp AI Agents: Inbound message payloads, user phone numbers, conversation timestamps, and query classification parameters.
    • AI Voice Calling Agents: Ephemeral audio streams, call duration metrics, speech-to-text transcripts, and structured sentiment/qualification tags.
    • Custom Knowledge Bases: Product catalogs, technical documentation, FAQs, and price sheets provided by clients for Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

verified 3. Lawful Basis & Purposes of Data Processing

We process your personal and business data strictly on established lawful grounds:

  • Performance of a Contract: To architect, build, host, and maintain custom web applications, deploy autonomous AI agents, and fulfill deliverables specified in our Statement of Work (SOW).
  • Legitimate Commercial Interests: To communicate regarding project updates, respond to inquiries, monitor server infrastructure against DDoS threats, and optimize system uptime.
  • Consent: Where you have explicitly opted in to receive technical newsletters, product updates, or marketing insights.
  • Legal & Statutory Obligations: Generating GST-compliant tax invoices, maintaining financial audit trails, and adhering to applicable Indian statutory requirements.

smart_toy 4. AI Data Governance & Zero Public-Model Training Guarantee

security Zero Public-Model Training Guarantee

Sociality AI guarantees that NONE of your proprietary business data, customer chat logs, voice call audio, or RAG documentation processed through our enterprise APIs will ever be used by us or third-party LLM providers (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta) to train, retrain, or improve public foundational AI models.

When we deploy autonomous AI chatbots, WhatsApp business automation, or AI voice agents:

  • Commercial Enterprise Endpoints: All AI processing is routed through commercial, enterprise-tier API endpoints governed by strict zero-data-retention (ZDR) and data-isolation terms.
  • Transient Memory: Conversational context is maintained only for the active session duration or pushed to your designated private database (e.g., Supabase PostgreSQL) with encrypted access.
  • Data Sanitization: Where requested, automated PII masking filters strip sensitive identification markers before prompt completion.

shield 6. Data Security Measures & Retention Lifecycles

We implement comprehensive technical and organizational safeguards in accordance with the IT Act, 2000 (Reasonable Security Practices):

  • Encryption Standards: All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3; all stored databases, backups, and credential vaults are encrypted using AES-256.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Access to client codebases, server dashboards, and AI configuration pipelines is restricted solely to authorized engineering personnel via Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
  • Retention Lifecycles:
    • Contact Submissions: Retained for up to 12 months for commercial correspondence.
    • AI Operational Logs: Maintained for 30 to 90 days for quality assurance and debugging before automatic deletion.
    • Tax & Billing Records: Retained for 7 years as required by Indian tax statutes.

account_circle 7. Your Rights (DPDP Act 2023, GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you are entitled to the following legal rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access & Summary: You have the right to request confirmation and a summary of personal data processed by Sociality AI.
  • Right to Correction & Updating: You may request correction of inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated personal data.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You may request the permanent deletion of your personal data, subject to statutory retention requirements.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw processing consent at any time by contacting our Grievance Officer.
  • Right to Nominate: Under the DPDP Act 2023, Indian citizens have the right to nominate another individual to exercise data rights in the event of death or incapacity.

cookie 8. Cookies & Local Storage Policy

Our website uses strictly functional and performance cookies to provide a smooth user experience:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for secure session persistence, CSRF protection, and UI navigation state.
  • Performance Telemetry: Anonymized scripts that track page speed, Core Web Vitals, and interaction bottlenecks.
  • Zero Ad-Network Trackers: We do NOT deploy third-party advertising cookies, behavioral re-targeting pixels, or sell user browsing history to data brokers.
